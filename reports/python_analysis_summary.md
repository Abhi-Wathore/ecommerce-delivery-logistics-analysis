**Missing Value Handling**

The orders dataset contains missing values in the approval, carrier handover, and customer delivery date columns. These values were not filled with estimated or artificial dates because the actual timestamps could not be confirmed. Instead, records with missing or invalid dates were excluded only from calculations that required those dates. This approach helps maintain the accuracy and reliability of the analysis.
